About Global ClockSync

Global ClockSync helps people who work across borders answer one question quickly: what time is it for everyone else, and when can we all meet?

What it does

  • Shows every city on one draggable timeline, with working hours, early/late hours and night colored differently.
  • Finds the overlap between everyone's working day and jumps to the best window.
  • Warns you before daylight saving changes shift the gap between two cities, and checks recurring meetings date by date.
  • Understands plain typing such as "3pm EST to IST" or "tomorrow 14:00 Kathmandu to New York".
  • Tracks whether the New York, London and Tokyo exchanges are open, with live countdowns.
  • Creates a copy-ready meeting invite, a Google Calendar link and a .ics file.

How it works

All calculations run in your browser using the built-in time zone database. There are no accounts and nothing you type is sent to our servers. The service is supported by advertising so it can stay free.

Accuracy

Time zone rules change when governments change them. Your device receives those updates with its normal software updates, so keep it current. Market hours and holidays are maintained by hand, so confirm with the exchange before you trade.
