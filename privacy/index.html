Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30. This policy explains what Global ClockSync ("we", "the site") does with information when you use this website.

Information we do not collect

Global ClockSync has no accounts, sign-up or forms that send data to us. Time zone calculations run entirely in your browser. We do not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

To save you time, the site stores your selected cities, working hours, theme and display preferences in your browser's local storage. A service worker caches the site's files so it can load offline. This data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising and cookies

The site is supported by advertising from third-party networks, including Adsterra. These partners may use cookies, device identifiers, your IP address and similar technologies to show and measure ads and to limit how often you see the same ad. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies, for example Adsterra's Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, ads load only after you click Accept in the notice. Everyone can change the choice at any time using "Ad and cookie settings" in the footer, and can also limit interest-based advertising through tools such as optout.aboutads.info (US), youronlinechoices.com (EU/UK), or a Global Privacy Control signal in your browser.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California, Canada or Australia) you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal data held by advertising partners. Please contact the relevant partner directly, or contact us and we will help point you in the right direction.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest revision.
