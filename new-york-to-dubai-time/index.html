    New York to Dubai time difference
    
    
    Dubai is 8 hours or 9 hours ahead of New York depending on daylight saving (and New York is 8 hours or 9 hours behind Dubai depending on daylight saving). From about Nov 1 to Mar 7 the gap is 9 hours; for the rest of the year it is 8 hours. Exact dates move by a few days each year.
    
    
    When daylight saving starts or ends, the gap moves by one hour, so a recurring meeting lands an hour earlier or later for one side. The converter above warns you up to 60 days ahead.
    
    
    Best time to meet
    
    
    There is no overlap between two 9 AM to 5 PM working days. With flexible hours (7 AM to 9 PM) you can meet from 7:00 AM to 1:00 PM in New York, which is 3:00 PM to 9:00 PM in Dubai. One side will usually work early or late.

    Frequently asked questions
    What is the time difference between New York and Dubai?
    Dubai is 8 hours or 9 hours ahead of New York depending on daylight saving. From about Nov 1 to Mar 7 the gap is 9 hours; for the rest of the year it is 8 hours. Exact dates move by a few days each year. This happens because one or both places use daylight saving time.
    What time is it in Dubai when it is 9 AM in New York?
    When it is 9:00 AM in New York, it is 5:00 PM in Dubai at the more common time difference.
    What is the best time for a meeting between New York and Dubai?
    There is no overlap between two 9 AM to 5 PM working days. With flexible hours (7 AM to 9 PM) you can meet from 7:00 AM to 1:00 PM in New York, which is 3:00 PM to 9:00 PM in Dubai. One side will usually work early or late.
    Does daylight saving time affect New York to Dubai?
    Yes. From about Nov 1 to Mar 7 the gap is 9 hours; for the rest of the year it is 8 hours. Exact dates move by a few days each year. Always re-check recurring meetings around those dates. The converter above shows an alert before the change happens.
