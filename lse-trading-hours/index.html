Frequently asked questions

What are the LSE trading hours?

London Stock Exchange regular trading runs from 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM exchange local time, Monday to Friday.

What time does LSE open in India?

In India (IST), the regular session runs winter: 1:30 PM to 10:00 PM; in the summer: 12:30 PM to 9:00 PM. The difference comes from daylight saving time.

What time does LSE open in Nepal?

In Nepal (NPT), the regular session runs winter: 1:45 PM to 10:15 PM; in the summer: 12:45 PM to 9:15 PM.

Is LSE open on public holidays?

No. LSE closes on its listed market holidays and some days close early. See the holiday list on this page, and always confirm with the exchange.
