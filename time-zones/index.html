How to read the table

The UTC offset is how far the place is ahead of (+) or behind (-) Coordinated Universal Time. Places that change their clocks show two offsets: one for mid January and one for mid July. The local time column updates every second from your device clock.

Use Live clock to add a place to your world clock, or Convert to start the time zone converter with that place as the base city. To compare two places directly, try a popular conversion such as New York to London or New York to India.

Frequently asked questions

What is an IANA time zone ID?

It is the standard name for a time zone region, such as America/New_York or Asia/Kolkata. The ID stays the same when the region changes its UTC offset for daylight saving time, so software uses IDs instead of fixed offsets.

What does "Clock changes: Yes" mean?

The place moves its clocks during the year, so its UTC offset is different in winter and summer. The table shows the offset in January and in July so you can see both.

What is the difference between UTC and GMT?

For everyday scheduling they are the same time. UTC is the modern global standard. GMT is the time zone used in the United Kingdom in winter.

Why is my city not listed?

The directory lists major cities and common alternatives, not every place. The converter and world clock can add any IANA time zone: type its name in the search box.
