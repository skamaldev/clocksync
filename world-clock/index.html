World Clock with Seconds

Live local time for cities around the world, updated every second. Add your own cities, reorder them and share the list with one link.

Your world clocks

You are viewing a shared list.

    Day means 6 AM to 6 PM local time, night is the rest. Real sunrise and sunset change with the season. Your list is saved only in this browser.

    How to use the world clock

    1. Type a city or country in the box and pick it from the list. Small typos are fine.
    2. Use the arrow buttons to reorder clocks and the cross to remove one.
    3. Each card shows the time with seconds, the date, the time zone abbreviation, the UTC offset and whether it is day or night there.
    4. Press Copy share link to send your list to a colleague.

    Need to plan a meeting instead of just reading the time? Open the time zone converter and meeting planner, or check when clocks change. Looking for one specific place? Use the time zone directory.

    Frequently asked questions

    How accurate is the world clock?

    It reads your device clock and applies each city's time zone rules from the IANA database built into your browser. If your device clock is wrong, every city is wrong by the same amount, so keep automatic time switched on.

    Are my cities saved?

    Yes, in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed. Clearing your browser data removes the list.

    How do I share my list?

    Press Copy share link. The link contains your cities. Whoever opens it sees your list and can press Save as my favourites to keep it; their own saved list is not changed until they do.

    Does it handle daylight saving time?

    Yes. Each clock follows the real rules of its city, so it moves automatically when that city changes its clocks. See the daylight saving page for upcoming dates.
