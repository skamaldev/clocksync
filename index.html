    How the score works

    Every person gets a penalty for the worst moment of the meeting: inside working hours 0, early morning (6 AM until work starts) 2, evening (work ends until 10 PM) 2, night (10 PM to 6 AM) 6. A weekend day adds 4.

    Slots are ranked by the smallest worst penalty first, then by the smallest total penalty, so one person is not sacrificed for the others. Results are at least two hours apart. Weekends are Saturday and Sunday except in Riyadh, Doha, Tel Aviv, Cairo and Dhaka (Friday and Saturday).

    The score is a guide, not a promise that everyone is happy. A slot is never called perfect.

    Frequently asked questions
    How do I convert a meeting time between time zones?
    Add the cities you need, then drag the timeline or the slider. Every city updates at the same moment, so you can read each local time directly without any arithmetic.
    How do I find overlapping working hours?
    Set your working hours, then check the Overlap bar. Bright green means everyone is inside working hours. Use Jump to best time to move the meeting straight there.
    Can I type a conversion instead of dragging?
    Yes. Use Quick convert and type something like "9 AM New York to London", "14:30 Tokyo to Los Angeles" or "10 AM London to New York next Monday". City names, country names and common abbreviations work. If an abbreviation such as IST, CST or BST can mean several places, the tool asks which one you mean instead of guessing.
    How does the Fair Meeting Time Finder choose a time?
    It tries every half hour in your date range and gives each person a penalty for early mornings, evenings, nights and weekends. It ranks slots by the smallest worst penalty first, shows the local time for everyone and explains who is outside normal hours. The scoring rules are listed under How the score works.
    Will my weekly meeting move after clocks change?
    Open the Recurring meeting check. It lists the next dates for the meeting and highlights any date where a city's local time shifts, so you can fix it before it happens.
    Does it handle daylight saving time?
    Yes. Times come from your browser's IANA time zone database, a warning box tells you about clock changes in the next 60 days, and the date picker lets you test any future date.
    Why does Kathmandu show a 45 minute offset?
    Nepal Time is UTC+5:45. A few places use offsets that are not whole hours, and the tool shows them exactly.
    Are the stock market hours exact?
    They show regular sessions and known holidays, but exchanges can change schedules without notice. Always confirm with the exchange before you trade.
    Is my data stored on a server?
    No. Everything is calculated in your browser. Your cities and preferences are saved only in your own browser storage.
