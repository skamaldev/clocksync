Frequently asked questions

When does daylight saving time start and end in 2026?

In the United States and Canada clocks go forward on March 8 and back on November 1. In the United Kingdom and the European Union they go forward on March 29 and back on October 25. In Sydney and Melbourne clocks went back on April 5 and go forward on October 4.

Which places do not change their clocks?

India, Nepal, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Japan, China, Singapore, the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, the Philippines, Indonesia, Thailand and Vietnam keep the same time all year. In the United States, Arizona (most of it) and Hawaii do not change either, and in Australia Queensland, the Northern Territory and Western Australia do not.

Why do meetings between the US and UK move by an hour for a few weeks?

The US and the UK change their clocks on different dates, so the usual 5 hour gap becomes 4 hours for about one to three weeks in spring and autumn. The tables below list those weeks for common pairs.

Does daylight saving time affect India or Nepal?

Not directly, because they do not change their clocks. But the gap to places that do change moves by one hour twice a year, so meetings with the US, UK or Australia still shift for you.

Where these dates come from

Dates are calculated from the IANA time zone database (version 2025c in the tool that built this page). Governments can change the rules at short notice, so check an official source for legal or contractual use. Pages are rebuilt when the database changes.
